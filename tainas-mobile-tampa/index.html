Our Story

Taina's Mobile Cigar Bar honors the heritage of the Taíno people — the indigenous Caribbean culture from which our name is drawn. We bring authentic, hand-rolled Puerto Rican cigars directly to events throughout the Tampa Bay area.

Every cigar we serve is curated from quality Caribbean growers, and every experience is hand-delivered by people who genuinely love this craft. Whether it's a wedding, a car meet, or a private celebration, we bring the lounge to you.
