Our Story

An Oasis Of Two Guilty Pleasures.

Founded by Brian Mitchell, Highland Cigar Co. has spent nearly two decades earning its reputation as the premier cigar and scotch lounge in Atlanta. Tucked into Inman Park on N. Highland Ave, the lounge combines a walk-in humidor with one of the deepest scotch selections in the Southeast — over 100 single malts and counting.

The room itself is built for the experience: comfortable leather sofas, high definition televisions, and a state-of-the-art ventilation system custom-engineered by Air Quality Sciences. The result is a cigar lounge where non-smoking friends can stay through the third Old Fashioned without flinching. The team, led by Dustin and the regulars they've grown alongside, has earned Best of Atlanta nine years running. They know what works.
