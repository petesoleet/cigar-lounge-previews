Our Story

A Refuge For The Smoker Of Fine Handmade Cigars.

Don Juan Cigar Co. was built on a single mission: create a space where the appreciation of a handmade cigar is taken seriously — without taking itself too seriously. From our Baton Rouge home in Towne Center, we've grown into Louisiana's most stocked humidor and most welcoming lounge.

Our team takes pride in helping every customer — first-timer or thirty-year aficionado — find the cigar that fits their palate. Add a full bar of whiskey, beer, and wine, leather chairs, wall-to-wall TVs, and the kind of regulars who'll save your seat. That's Don Juan.
