Our Story

From The Mill to the Community

You do not even have to smoke a cigar. Everyone is welcome and we do not want anyone to feel intimidated. — GM Destiny Williams

Canton Cigar Company opened in the historic Mill on Etowah with a single idea — that a great cigar lounge should feel like everyone's favorite living room. Pet friendly. Food truck friendly. People friendly. High ceilings, a massive patio, a walk-in humidor, and a craft cocktail bar where the bartenders know your name. This is North Georgia's social destination.
