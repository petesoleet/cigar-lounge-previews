Our Story

Good Vibes Only. Family Built. Fort Mill Proud.

I came in randomly and was not disappointed. This is my new solo and small group hangout spot — and I will be returning to get a membership.

The Royal Cigar Bar and Bistro was built on a simple belief — that a cigar lounge should feel like home from the first visit. David and the family team brought Fort Mill something it did not know it needed: a premium cigar bar with live music, incredible food, and a community built on warmth and good energy. Conveniently located on Springhill Farm Road just minutes from the North Carolina state line, The Royal has become the destination for cigar lovers across both Carolinas.
