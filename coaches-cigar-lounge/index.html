Our Story

Built by Coaches. Made for Everyone.

We spent years coaching players to perform under pressure. Now we are building a space where pressure does not exist.

Russel Blue and Bill Honeck spent decades on the sidelines coaching players to push past limits. When they imagined their next chapter it looked like a walk-in humidor, leather chairs, and a room where the game on TV is background noise to good conversation. Located on historic Main Street in Tomball - Coaches is your third place.
