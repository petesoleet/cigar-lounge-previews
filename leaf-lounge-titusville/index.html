Our Story

Titusville's Best-Kept Secret

This is definitely a lounge for the community. We need more places like this where we feel at home like family.

The Leaf Lounge opened on South Washington Avenue in Titusville with a clear mission — create a welcoming space where the Space Coast community could gather, smoke great cigars, eat great wings, and feel genuinely at home. With 176 reviews averaging 4.9 stars, it is clear they got it right. A walk-in humidor stocked with premium and boutique brands, craft cocktails, cold beer, pool tables, and a staff that treats everyone like they have been coming for years.
