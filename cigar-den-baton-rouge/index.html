Our Story

20 Years Of Cigar Knowledge — Rolled Into One Den.

The Cigar Den opened in 2020, but the man behind it brings two decades of knowing exactly what a cigar enthusiast wants when they walk in the door. Top-notch ventilation. A humidor that's growing every month. Tobacco-row portraits on the wall. And the rare blends most Baton Rouge shops just don't carry.

— Ricky Thibodeaux, Owner
