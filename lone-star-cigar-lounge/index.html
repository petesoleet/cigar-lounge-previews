Our Story

Chris Built Something Worth Showing Up For

Everyone is family here.

When Chris opened Lone Star in Helotes he was not building a smoke shop - he was building a community. Situated at 14743 Old Bandera Road, just minutes from Bexar County, the lounge drew regulars from across San Antonio almost immediately. Whether you are a first-timer or a regular who brings friends from out of town, you will be greeted by name every single time.
