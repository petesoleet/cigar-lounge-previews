Our Story

McDonough's First Bank.
Now Its Premier Cigar Lounge.

The Cigar Bank occupies a building with a century of stories. Built in 1911 as McDonough's premier banking institution, the original marble columns, intricate moldings, vintage teller windows, and majestic vault have all been preserved — and reimagined for a different kind of clientele.

"We do more than offer premium cigars. We curate experiences that elevate your appreciation for the finer things."

Today, the vault holds humidified safety deposit boxes for Atlanta-area aficionados. The teller windows are now where you order your Old Fashioned. And the historic marble lobby hosts Smoke & Keys jazz nights, Sommelier Saturdays, and Room 101 cigar releases. Every visit is more than a smoke. It's an experience that transports guests to a timeless world of sophistication and refinement.
