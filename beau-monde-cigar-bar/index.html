Our Story

A Privately Owned Cigar Bar — Run By People Who Actually Know Your Name

Tucked off Jones Bridge Road, Beau Monde was built on a simple idea: a neighborhood cigar bar where the staff remembers your drink, the regulars welcome out-of-towners like family, and the cigar selection earns its place in the humidor.

What started as a quiet patio with a great Old Fashioned has grown into Alpharetta's go-to for live music nights, smoked bourbon flights, and the wings people drive 35 minutes for. Five TVs in the bar. The whole NFL slate on Sundays. And the kind of staff that asks your name — and remembers it.
