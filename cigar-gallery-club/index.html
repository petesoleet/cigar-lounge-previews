Our Story

A Different Era. A Different Vibe. Kennesaw's Cigar Living Room.

Tucked off Cherokee Street, the Cigar Gallery Club was built around a simple belief: a cigar lounge should be more than a humidor. It should be the place where your favorite band plays, your whisky is already poured, and the staff knows what you smoke before you ask.

Mitch and the team have curated one of the largest boutique cigar selections in all of Georgia — including Fuente OpusX, Stolen Throne, and rare blends you won't find anywhere else within an hour of Atlanta. Pair that with a kitchen, a full bar, weekly live music, and a humidor that members trust with their personal stash, and you've got the Gallery.
