Our Story

Since opening our doors in the heart of downtown Miami, City Cigar Lounge has been the sanctuary where Miami's most discerning professionals and enthusiasts gather. Our 350+ five-star reviews speak to the experience we've crafted: where classic elegance meets modern luxury.

We've cultivated more than just a lounge—we've built a community. From our temperature-controlled humidor stocked with rare finds to our leather seating overlooking the Miami skyline, every detail reflects our commitment to excellence.

Whether you're closing a deal, celebrating success, or simply savoring a quiet evening with a exceptional smoke, City Cigar Lounge is your destination.
