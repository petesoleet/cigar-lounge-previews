Our Story

From Vegas To The French Quarter — With Better Cigars.

When Andrew Wilson left a career running marketing for MGM Resorts in Las Vegas, he had one mission: bring Sin City's sophisticated cigar culture to the city he loved most. The result is Cuban Creations — the French Quarter's only full-service cigar bar where smoking indoors is grandfathered in, the leather couches are deep, and the Sazerac is the bestseller.

"There is a big history of cigars in New Orleans. We are trying to bring back the traditional quality in a nicer setting."

What was once a swanky bath house — pool and all — has become a haven of leather, hand-carved tables, vintage Cuban posters, and a humidor stocked with Cuban-seed cigars from Nicaragua. Three domino tables. A canopied sidewalk lounge. A BBQ smoker. And the kind of crowd where tourists become regulars before their flight home.
