Our Story

Built Around a Wooden Indian.
And a Family.

Owner Chad Miller spent 20 years in the energy industry before opening The Chief. The name came from Old Chief Woodenhead — a wooden cigar store Indian that belonged to his father, lost for decades, and recovered just before Chad opened the lounge. The Chief has stood proudly over the lounge ever since, watching over a Miller family business in the Wise County town it calls home.

"Wise County didn't have anything that combined the high-quality cigars and decor of a premium lounge with the music, lighting, and vibe of a great dive bar. So that's what I'd shoot for."

The result is dive bar chic — quality cigars, a fully stocked walk-in humidor, an Old Fashioned with a devout following, and a community of regulars who keep showing up. Christus Rex.
