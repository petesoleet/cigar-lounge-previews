Our Story

Kevin and Manny Built Brookhaven a Home

Once the bar and VIP section in the back opens up it will be even better. But honestly — it is already the best.

Kevin and Emmanuel Peguero did not set out to open a cigar shop. They set out to create a sanctuary. Located at 4276 Peachtree Road NE in Brookhaven, La Casa del Tabaco Social quickly became the neighborhood's most talked-about secret. With a 4.9-star rating and a loyal following that treats the lounge like their living room, La Casa is proof that intimacy and excellence are not mutually exclusive.
