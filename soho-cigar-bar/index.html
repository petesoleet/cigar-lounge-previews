Our Story

A Refuge The City Tried To Lose. We're Still Here.

Originally opened in 1998 as Circa Tabac and reborn in 2014 as Soho Cigar Bar, we are one of the few establishments in New York City that legally permits indoor smoking. The renovation kept the 1920s soul — plush leather, art deco, candle-lit cocktail tables — while adding one of the best ventilation systems in Manhattan.

"Money wasn't a consideration when I put it in. We ended up installing twice as many ventilation systems as required."

The cigar menu is broken into full, sweet, spicy, and light. The whiskey list runs over 150 deep — from Laphroaig 10 to Dalmore 25 to Balvenie 30. The crowd skews younger and more diverse than you'd expect from a cigar bar — because Soho was built for them. After-work cocktails. Friday night birthdays. Sunday football. The last refuge in a city that's banned indoor everything.
