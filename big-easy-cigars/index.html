Our Story

An Opulent Respite From The Hustle.

Big Easy Cigars is the lounge greater New Orleans built for the discerning — a luxury high-end cigar destination tucked into the bustling community of Metairie. The walls are adorned with cigar artwork. The leather seating is plush. The humidor stocks the finest from boutique manufacturers and the names everyone knows.

Our team — knowledgeable cigar aficionados the regulars know by name — exists to help you find the perfect stick. Whether you're a seasoned connoisseur or trying your first cigar, the welcome you'll receive at Big Easy is the reason customers drive in from across the parish.
