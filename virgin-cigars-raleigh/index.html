Our Story

Built for the Triangle's Best

Virgin Cigars was built around a single conviction: Raleigh's cigar community deserved a lounge that took the experience as seriously as they do. The No. 11 Lounge is the result. A premium walk-in humidor, a dedicated smoking space, and a membership system designed to reward the regulars who make the community what it is.
